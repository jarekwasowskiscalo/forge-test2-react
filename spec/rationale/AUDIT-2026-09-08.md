## What was asked

A thorough and deliberately critical audit of two things: the **flow** — the SDD process, its
orchestrator, the 42 skills, the `_shared/sdd` library, the hooks and the scripts — and the
**state** — the template itself: the application, the tests, the specification, the contracts,
the documentation, CI and the infrastructure. The questions were whether everything is
coherent, whether the parts cooperate, and whether anything was left behind.

## Method

Three independent read-only explorers, each over one area (the framework under `.claude/` and
`.specconf/`; the product, scripts, CI and infrastructure; the specification, contracts and
documentation), each returning claims with `file:line` evidence — after which **every claim was
verified against the source before being written here**, and thirteen were corrected or dropped
in the process (three counted as consistent, the rest re-scoped). **The audit's own diff was then
reviewed the same way**, on the day after, which is where F-104 comes from: the register below is
not only about the tree this branch started from. Plus measurements: every
suite that runs without a container or an application, the content gates, the skill lint, the
contract check, the generated types, hygiene, the dependency audit, Terraform validation through
the pinned image, the hooks probed live, and CI's own verdict on the trunk.

Since the previous audit the trunk took five large refactors — the gate rename (2026-09-06), the
recut of the tree by bounded context (2026-09-07), the operator documentation under `docs/`,
the CI reporter, and the retro as a beat of every stage boundary (merged the morning of this
audit). Each is the classic source of leftovers, and each left some.

**What could not be run on this machine:** nothing that matters. The e2e suite and the image
build were not run locally — the first writes into the developer's database volume and the
second is CI's `image` job — and both were green on the trunk's last CI run (`34266839410`).
