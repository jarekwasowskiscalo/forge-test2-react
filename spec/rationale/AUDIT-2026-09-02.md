# Audit of `sdd-app-template` — 2026-09-02

**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Starting revision:** `da2c734` + 165 uncommitted files ·
**Branch:** `main` · **Author:** Claude Fable 5.1 (a Claude Code session) · **Previous
audit:** [`AUDIT-2026-09-01.md`](AUDIT-2026-09-01.md), of whose 22 findings 18 were closed

This document is dated and is not edited after the fact. It describes the tree on the day of
the audit and what was fixed on the same day — and separately, what could not be fixed.

## Method

Three independent reviews (the `.claude/` and `.specconf/` framework; the product, the
scripts, the infrastructure and CI; the specification, the contracts and the documentation),
each ending in a list of claims with `file:line`, after which **every claim was verified
against the source** before being written here. Plus measurements: `lint.sh`,
`test.sh --no-db`, `test.sh frontend`, `specs.sh`, `sdd-tests.sh`, `generate.sh --check`,
`contracts.sh`, `gate.py run --profile boundary-no-docker`.

**What could not be run on this machine:** the Docker daemon was down, and `terraform`,
`shellcheck` and `actionlint` were not installed. The integration suite (68 tests), e2e (19
scenarios + 14 smokes), HCL validation, the script lint and the workflow lint — **from
reading**, not from running. Every sentence about them below carries that caveat.

## Decisions taken in this audit (by the user)

1. **The 11 historical ADRs go; the ADR mechanism stays.** The content of each decision — with
   its date, its reason and its rejected alternatives — now stands in the normative document
   that binds: `data-model.md` (owner of the schema, identifiers), `conventions.md` (language,
   scripts, the ADR threshold), `testing.md` (the UI smoke), `architecture.md` (one engine,
   deployment, three environments), `contracts/README.md` (a boundary as a contract). Around 200
   `ADR-00xx` citations across ~70 files were repointed at those sections; `spec/ADR/` is empty
   until the first change through `/sdd`, and three framework tests learned an empty corpus.
2. Fixes in `app/`, `frontend/src/` and `alembic/` straight onto `main`, with no change record.
3. Phrase search without an index, as a decision with a threshold.
4. The whole working tree cut into thematic commits, without a push.
