# Why not <alternative>

A rationale note: reasoning worth keeping that is **not** a decision. If it has a date, a
decision and a rejected alternative, it is an ADR and belongs in [`../ADR/`](../ADR/) — an
ADR binds, this does not.

Use this shape for:

- a planning conversation whose individual decisions have ADRs of their own, kept because the
  *context* explains several of them at once;
- a question that comes back regularly and has a settled answer nobody wrote down;
- an approach that was tried and abandoned, where the abandonment is expensive knowledge.

## What was considered

The alternative, presented honestly enough that somebody who prefers it would recognise it. A
rationale note that makes a straw man of its alternative is worse than none, because the next
person will derive the real version and conclude that nobody thought of it.

## Why not

The reason, tied to something concrete: a constraint, a measurement, an incident. "It seemed
wrong" is not a reason anybody can come back to.

## What would change it

The condition under which the answer reverses. This is the paragraph that makes a rationale
note useful two years later — without it a reader cannot tell a settled question from a stale
one.
